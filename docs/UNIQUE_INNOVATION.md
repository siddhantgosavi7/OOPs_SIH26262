# Unique Innovation (and what we do *not* claim)

> We do not claim any of D1–D5 is unique to us. Many SIH teams will build workflow + SLA + rule engine + audit chain; we treat those as **table stakes** and make them verifiable. Update this file after each competitor re-check.

| # | Differentiator | Why it matters | Evidence we will show | Honest caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Process-mining conformance & bottleneck root cause | Explains *why* cases stall, not just how many | Injected deviations detected / total on synthetic logs | Synthetic logs |
| D2 | Capacity what-if simulator | Test officer allocation / SLA changes before acting | Scenario table with confidence intervals and visible parameters | Illustrative until calibrated |
| D3 | Verifiable approval credential + public check | Anyone can verify a record; tampering is detectable | Tampered record fails verification | Demo keys; not an AICTE credential |
| D4 | Institution obligation graph | Shows which approvals are at risk when a lease/NOC/undertaking lapses | Seeded expiring-lease scenario | Edges are modelled, not official |
| D5 | Explainability everywhere | Trust and contestability | Every finding shows rule ID, version, evidence, clause | Clause refs only where verified |

## Where we are deliberately *not* innovating
Login, RBAC, notifications, CSV export. These are supporting features.
